Experience

I am a UI/UX Designer with 4+ years of experience creating digital products for banking, fintech, and healthcare. My work covers mobile apps, responsive web platforms, KYC flows, lending experiences, dashboards, and design systems.

I use research, journey mapping, information architecture, and high-fidelity prototyping to turn product goals into clear, accessible user experiences.

My approach combines business context with scalable UI systems, helping teams reduce onboarding friction, improve engagement, and deliver with confidence.

Currently a Product Designer at Postpi Technology Pvt. Ltd. (Oct 2023–Present), previously a UX/UI Designer at Crocus Global Services (Oct 2022–Oct 2023), with freelance project work from 2020–2022. I hold a B.Com in Computer Applications from Vikrama Simhapuri University.

Strong experiences are built on evidence, clarity, and care.

Design Focus

I design more than attractive interfaces. I focus on whether users can understand a task quickly, complete it confidently, and receive the right feedback at every step.

For complex products, I identify the critical path first—then align business goals, user needs, and visual hierarchy to make that path feel simple.

Specialist In

Figma, design systems, user research, wireframing, prototyping, and mobile-first product design.
